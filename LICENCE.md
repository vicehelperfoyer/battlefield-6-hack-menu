## 🧩 About

**Battlefield 6 overlay** is a curated list of Battlefield 6 open-source tools:

- **Overlays** — on-screen helpers, layout notes, HUD framing, menu toggles
- **Quality-of-Life Helpers** — loadout catalog, keybind manager, overlay notes
- **Research Notes** — practice ranges and config notes for educational use

Based on publicly available community tools and overlay helpers.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Practice Helpers
- Menu Overlay — quick access to helper panels
- HUD Notes — visualize frame and status information
- Replay Notes — record and review offline sessions
- Playback Speed — adjust training playback for study (0.1x to 5x)

### 🎨 Catalog & Loadouts
- Loadout Catalog — browse weapons, attachments, and cosmetics
- Custom Preset Notes — organize player loadouts
- Team Colors — improved visibility for study layouts

### 🏋️ Practice Layout
- Training Notes — segment study on any map
- Camera Control — manually adjust view angle in offline practice
- Movement Buffer Notes — chain maneuvers in the training range
